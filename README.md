# Evidence-Based Prevention Strategies for Stress Fractures and Tendinopathy in Female Football Players

[![ClinicalTrials.gov Data Synthesized](https://img.shields.io/badge/ClinicalTrials.gov-Synthesized-00457C.svg)](https://clinicaltrials.gov/)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

A comprehensive clinical protocol and evidence-based guideline repository for preventing **bone stress injuries (stress fractures)** and **tendinopathies (patellar and Achilles)** in female football (soccer) players. This guide synthesizes recent clinical trial evidence registered on [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/) alongside consensus statements from sports medicine literature.

## 🛡️ The 4 Pillars of Prevention

1. **Nutritional Periodization & RED-S Prevention:**
   - Target Energy Availability: $> 45\text{ kcal/kg FFM/day}$.
   - High carbohydrate availability ($6\text{--}8\text{ g/kg/day}$) and distributed protein ($1.6\text{--}2.0\text{ g/kg/day}$).
   - Calcium ($1200\text{--}1500\text{ mg/day}$), Vitamin D3 (target serum $> 75\text{ nmol/L}$), and pre-loading collagen ($15\text{ g}$ hydrolyzed collagen + Vitamin C, 45 min pre-training).
   - Semi-annual LEAF-Q and menstrual regularity screening.

2. **Mechanical Load & Pitch/Footwear Management:**
   - Maintain Acute-to-Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR) in the **$0.8\text{--}1.3$** zone.
   - Enforce 48–72 hour tendon recovery windows following high stretch-shortening cycle (SSC) exposures.
   - Utilize conical/shorter stud configurations on artificial turf to limit torsional stress on the 5th metatarsal and ankle.

3. **Neuromuscular Control, Landing Biomechanics & Heavy Slow Resistance (HSR):**
   - FIFA 11+ integration before all training sessions (focus on core, gluteus medius, and soft-landing mechanics).
   - Heavy Slow Resistance (3s up / 3s down, $70\text{--}85\%\text{ 1RM}$) for quadriceps, gastrocnemius, and soleus.
   - Osteogenic micro-dosing: 10–20 high-impact multi-directional jumps twice weekly when energy-replete.

4. **Early Detection & Clinical Triage:**
   - Weekly monitoring of morning stiffness duration, single-leg decline squats, and localized bone tenderness.
   - Rapid off-loading protocol at the reactive stage before irreversible structural damage or full cortical fracture occurs.
